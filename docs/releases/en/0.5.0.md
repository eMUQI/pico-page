## Added

- Added an offline dictionary with support for StarDict dictionaries and compressed dictionary data. Enable, reorder, or switch between multiple sources. You can look up successive words, adjust the selection by word or by character, and expand definitions.
- Added a first-use guide where you can practice turning pages. You can reopen it from Help.

## Improved

- Reduced repeated work when reading across multiple chapter or section boundaries, improving page turn response. Repeated dictionary lookups also take less time.
- The library and in-book lists such as contents and bookmarks use the reading font. Adjusted button hierarchy, spacing, and touch areas.
- The device shows a waiting message before clearing the cache or calculating storage usage. You can cancel cache clearing. Your books, settings, and reading progress stay on the card.
- The web installer shows status during download, erase, and verification. It reports when writing is complete separately from when the device starts.

## Fixed

- Fixed a case where some valid book covers failed to load for the sleep screen. Improved handling when the cache is cold, memory is limited, or a read fails temporarily.
- Fixed an issue where opening book details in the library still required lifting your finger after the long-press threshold.
- Fixed problems switching dictionary sources, adjusting the selected text, and showing definitions. Improved feedback when there are no results or a read fails.
- Fixed layout edits being lost after you canceled a font preview.
- Fixed some instances of footer ghosting after a page turn, issues with touch response, and problems restoring peripherals around sleep.
- Improved reading of UTF-16 TXT files with a BOM and some EPUB content. The device now distinguishes a missing card, an empty library, and a read failure.
- Fixed resource handoff when Wi-Fi transfer ends or is canceled.

## Notes

- The firmware is still in testing and features may change.
- Install or update firmware only from picopage.wulu.zone. The device cannot tell whether firmware comes from the official source. Firmware from another source may be unsafe.
