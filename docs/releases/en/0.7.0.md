## Upgrade

- **Upgrading from 0.6.2 or earlier to 0.7.0 requires a one-time reinstall from a computer.** This release uses two 7 MiB firmware partitions. Until you reinstall, **Firmware update** on the device offers at most 0.6.2 and may still show **Already up to date**.
- **Connect the Read Pico with a USB data cable.** Open the [official installer](https://picopage.wulu.zone/en/install/) in Chrome or Edge on your computer and follow the prompts to install 0.7.0.

## Changed

- Added Traditional Chinese and English interfaces. You can choose a language on first startup and switch languages later in Settings.
- In **Publisher layout** for English EPUBs, when the book does not specify alignment, full lines of body text are justified by default and the last line of each paragraph stays ragged. For English TXT files, choose **Reflow paragraphs** for each book to join line breaks within paragraphs.
- The cover sleep screen shows the current chapter and reading position. When the device goes to sleep from the library or Settings, it shows the built-in library illustration. The sleep screen prompts you to charge when the battery is low.
- The reading toolbar shows the time and battery level at the top. Notices about differences between the two devices’ reading positions now appear in the Sync panel.

## Fixed

- Corrected the progress shown on the cover sleep screen and in the reading toolbar for some pages. The chapter name on the sleep screen now uses the current book's reading font.
