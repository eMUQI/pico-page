## Upgrade

- On 0.7.0, you can upgrade to 0.8.0 from **Firmware update** on the device.
- **On 0.6.2 or earlier, you need a one-time reinstall from a computer.** Connect the Read Pico with a USB data cable, open the [official installer](https://picopage.wulu.zone/en/install/) in Chrome or Edge on your computer, and follow the prompts to install 0.8.0.

## Added

- Added **Diagonal sweep** to **Page-turn effect**. The next page unfolds from the bottom-right corner and the previous page from the bottom-left corner.
- Added **Follow touch** to **Page-turn effect**. When you tap the upper or lower part of the page to turn it, the page sweeps from the matching corner; a tap in the middle unfolds it horizontally. Swipes use the point where your finger first touched, and button presses unfold horizontally.
- Added **Weight** to **Publisher layout** for EPUBs, with **Lighter**, **Book style** and **Heavier**. The book's bold text keeps its contrast.
- Added **Align** to **Uniform layout** for EPUBs and to TXT files, with **Left** (the default) and **Justified**.
- Added **Clock** to the **Sync** panel. It shows the current time and the last calibration; tap **Calibrate now** to set the time over the network. You can also calibrate from **Settings → Wi-Fi**.

## Changed

- **Ripple** and **Diagonal sweep** now use an eight-level grayscale waveform, so drawing each page turn takes about 0.1 s less. With a page-turn effect on, images refreshed with the text also use eight gray levels.

## Fixed

- Fixed **Publisher layout** dropping leading spaces or the book's first-line indent.
- Fixed long TXT files indenting the rest of a paragraph as a new paragraph when it crossed an internal segment.
